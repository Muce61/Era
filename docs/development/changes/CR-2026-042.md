## Decision

S2P13-T11 uses the following minimum historical price-only lifecycle producer:

- protection and structure exits are explicitly `NOT_MODELLED_STAGE2`; they are not encoded as
  observed `false` facts and cannot trigger an exit;
- Contract Price 1s drives scenario net PnL, activation, near-zero, theoretical liquidation and
  funding notional;
- canonical Trades drive target and stop crossing in stable
  `(ts_event_ns, venue_trade_id, canonical_trade_id)` order;
- at the same event second, Contract Price risk/liquidation state is processed before Trade stop
  and target crossings;
- proxy quantity is fixed at `800 USDT / entry_price`; each historical funding settlement amount
  is `fixed_quantity × settlement Contract Price × signed historical funding rate`;
- immediate-exit and continue-holding policies replay the same ordered Episode stream as separate
  single-position timelines; an Episode arriving while that policy is occupied is skipped;
- a right-censored position is not assumed flat, so that policy admits no later Episode.

These are H3 historical conditional proxy rules, not historical execution or a Stage 3 result.
