## Decision

S2P13-T11 publishes two separate funding tracks:

1. `PRIMARY_HISTORICAL_ACTUAL`: accumulate the signed funding cash flows at their real historical
   settlement timestamps. Positive values are long payments and negative values are receipts.
2. `STRESS_ADVERSE_1_5X` and `STRESS_ADVERSE_2X`: multiply historical positive payments by 1.5 or
   2, never amplify historical receipts, and also report `STRESS_NO_FUNDING_CREDIT`, where negative
   receipts are capped at zero.

Stress evidence cannot substitute for a missing Primary row, cannot close the historical-data gate
and cannot be described as historical net PnL.

The theoretical liquidation proxy no longer consumes Mark Price or a historical exchange bracket.
On the Contract Price/canonical Trade H3 path it records
`SCENARIO_LIQUIDATION_BOUNDARY_CROSSED` when scenario net PnL reaches `-8U`, equivalently when the
10U ticket reaches the reserved 2U boundary. This is a margin-depletion scenario, not Binance
historical liquidation.

## Unchanged gates

Primary execution still requires immutable BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT historical funding rows with source,
timestamp, sign, availability, gap and Hash evidence. OQ-S2-009/010/011, the final-code seven-day
rehearsal and the commit-bound human receipt continue to block formal Authority, Run and publish.
