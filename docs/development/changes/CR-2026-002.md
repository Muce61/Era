dependencies and preregistration gate, but did not fully define causal key-level formulas,
event thresholds, G1/G3/G4 formulas, the OFAT parameter family or the single full-run CLI.

`ADR-S2-005-event-construction-baseline.md`. The Stage 2 Plan remains v1.2. Group 1 Tasks
S2-T19 and S2-T01 through S2-T10 advance to v1.3, keep their approved dependency graph and
scope, and bind the new decision. Groups 2 through 4 remain DRAFT and unchanged.

L3: this freezes a preregistered research method and CLI for Group 1 without changing the
V1.3.4 formal specification, Stage 1 contracts, risk behavior or execution behavior. The values
are not optimal and are not FROZEN live rules.

## Validation and invalidation

Group 1 must revalidate schemas, causal boundaries, stable identity, parameter isolation,
append-only publication, recovery and deterministic rebuild. Any change to a bound formula,
threshold, parameter family, identity rule, data baseline or CLI requires a new approved change
and preregistration before execution. Existing manifests and failed evidence remain append-only.
