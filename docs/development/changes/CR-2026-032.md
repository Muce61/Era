## Requested direction

Preserve event research as immutable, exit-rule-free, multi-window raw paths. Separately, require a
complete-strategy research replay to continue from theoretical entry until theoretical full
closure rather than ending at an arbitrary event window.

The following sub-hypothesis is recorded verbatim as a proposed preregistration:

> 事件在存活较长时间、尚未激活、且净可退出 PnL 接近零时，后续目标优先概率和净期望是否提高？

Interpretation is fixed before results:

- an increase supports a delayed-activation or compression-then-breakout phenomenon and suggests
  the current 8-minute exit may be too early;
- a decrease supports momentum decay and gives a time stop a research basis;
- improvement only within preregistered volatility or higher-timeframe states supports a future
  conditional time rule rather than one universal 8/15/25-minute rule.

None of these outcomes automatically changes a strategy rule. The 8/15/25-minute values remain
RESEARCH baselines and cannot be frozen from one retrospective result.

## Layer A — raw event research

- Preserve the accepted T11 H1/H2 paths from `MarketEpisode.available_at_ts` over the registered
  T1-T4 windows.
- Raw path rows contain observed Contract Price/Trade facts and lineage only. Target, stop, time
  exit, profit protection or theoretical position state must not rewrite or truncate them.
- T12 metrics and T13/T14 labels remain derived views. They do not mutate the raw path.
- T1-T4 remain append-only historical evidence. A future lifecycle study cannot be used to rescue
  or relabel the already registered sole Primary T2 result.

## Layer B — complete-strategy lifecycle research

- Create a separate variable-length lifecycle beginning at the preregistered theoretical entry and
  ending only at `THEORETICAL_FULLY_FLAT`, or at a preregistered right-censor boundary that is
  reported as not fully closed.
- Stage 2/H2 may record only price-path and decision facts. It cannot claim executable net PnL,
  fills, partial fills, real slippage or real flat state.
- Net exitable PnL and theoretical full closure require an H3 scenario with frozen fees, funding,
  latency, entry/exit slippage, partial-fill and residual-quantity rules. The research field must be
  named `scenario_net_exitable_pnl`; it must not reuse the F1/live field
  `estimated_exit_net_pnl`.
- The theoretical terminal name must not be `POSITION_FLAT`. That term remains reserved for the
  real PositionClosureProtocol after all venue positions and related orders are proven terminal.
- Every theoretical exit leg must reduce remaining proxy quantity, and closure is reached only
  when proxy quantity is exactly zero under the frozen Decimal contract.

## Proposed landmark sub-study

Use preregistered landmark analysis to avoid survivor and future-information bias:

1. At each frozen landmark time, form the risk set from lifecycles still alive at that instant.
2. Use only information available by the landmark to determine activation state, net-exitable-PnL
   band, volatility state and higher-timeframe state.
3. Compare qualified near-zero/not-activated survivors with an outcome-blind matched risk set from
   the same instrument, period, fold, parameter set and lifecycle age.
4. Measure subsequent target-first probability and H3 scenario net expectancy from the landmark
   to theoretical closure or the frozen censor boundary.
5. Report every preregistered landmark and subgroup. Do not select the most favorable time or state
   after reading results.

## Values that must be frozen before implementation

OQ-S2-010 remains open until human approval defines:

- the landmark/survival times and which one, if any, is Primary;
- the exact `NOT_ACTIVATED` state machine and activation threshold;
- the H3 net-exitable-PnL formula and symmetric/asymmetric near-zero band;
- the comparison cohort at each landmark and the exact meaning of “提高”;
- theoretical entry, quantity, leverage, liquidation-buffer and full-closure contracts;
- fees, funding, latency, slippage, partial-fill and residual-quantity scenarios;
- target-priority outcome, whether thresholds remain anchored to entry or landmark, expectancy
  formula, exit priority and tie semantics;
- maximum lifecycle/censor horizon and handling of data end, source gaps and still-open paths;
- the preregistered volatility/higher-timeframe subgroup set and multiplicity control;
- whether this is an exploratory Stage 2 addendum or a new Stage 3 H3 Task.
