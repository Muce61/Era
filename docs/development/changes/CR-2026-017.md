## Trigger

The unique CR-2026-016 successor Run B
`stage2-g1-v2-b-20260720T111704Z-9c4b7c423a04` adopted 8,708 authorized monthly-result files,
completed 19,008 Foundation and 61,776 Group-1 logical partitions, recreated 44 Group-1 packed
objects and entered release with all six Task receipts sealed. Release then failed unpublished
while `CatalogPublisherV2` combined 164 Foundation plus 44 Group-1 objects and Seals. The
publisher retained a terminal `<= 200` summary-count check and rejected the real total of 208.

This is the exact object-count hard-failure semantics already superseded by approved
[CR-2026-013](CR-2026-013.md). CR-2026-013 requires object-count thresholds to produce resource
observation evidence without changing semantic publication quality. Duplicate, ownership, Hash,
Schema, Receipt, Seal, snapshot and append-only checks all passed before the stale count gate.

## Candidate correction

1. Remove only the stale `CatalogPublisherV2` terminal `<= 200` object/Seal summary-count check.
2. Preserve every semantic and integrity check, including exact component coverage, duplicate
   artifacts/Seals, cross-component ownership, physical SHA-256, Schema, Receipt, Seal, Snapshot
   and atomic publication checks.
3. Keep object and Seal counts in the sealed Catalog indexes and publication quality evidence.
4. Add a real publisher regression with 201 distinct objects and Seals; do not validate only the
   quality-report model.
5. Keep the failed successor immutable and unpublished. It may not resume or publish under a
   changed code tree. Any approved successor may adopt only fully verified monthly Foundation and
   Group-1 evidence and must recreate final packing, release, verify and exact comparison.
