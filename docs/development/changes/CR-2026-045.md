## Problem

The completed formal T16 chain is mechanically complete, but its control reader promoted any
day-level Stage 1 venue-ID anomaly into a gap for every selected H2 window on that UTC day. This
made `SOURCE_GAP_BEFORE_DECISION` affect 1,257,019 of 1,278,527 control matrices while the event
side affected 96,922 of 413,837 eligible matrices. The resulting event-minus-baseline deltas are
not acceptable research evidence.

## Decision

- Preserve the completed chain, Catalog, Manifest, Verify and all historical failure evidence.
- Record that chain as `ENGINEERING_PASS / RESEARCH_REJECTED_COVERAGE_ASYMMETRY`.
- Use `H2_WINDOW_INTERNAL_GAP_BEFORE_DECISION_V1` for both event and control semantics.
- Detect a control gap only between adjacent stable-ordered facts inside the exact H2 window.
- A gap invalidates a cell only when it precedes that cell's observed decision or requested
  horizon end. A target/stop observed before a later gap remains observed.
- Same-venue-ID conflict facts remain separate facts and are not converted into missing data.
- Daily Stage 1 quality aggregates remain required source-binding evidence, but cannot label every
  intraday window as missing.
- New summaries, post-selection reports and independent Verify must expose event/control
  gap-affected counts and rates under the same coverage contract.
- Web UI must project the active formal chain as the top-level state and honor an explicit
  CR-2026-044 rehearsal waiver.

## Boundary

This change does not modify Stage 1, T10–T15 sealed evidence, `docs/spec/**`, bins, matching,
registered combinations or research thresholds. It does not authorize a new Authority or Run.
A future T16-only successor requires clean-code validation and separate human approval. T17+ and
Stage 3 remain forbidden.
