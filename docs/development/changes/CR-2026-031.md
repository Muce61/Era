## Evidence-backed problem

Stage 1 currently increments `venue_trade_id_gap_count` whenever two adjacent normalized public
Trades have numeric IDs `N` and `M` with `M > N + 1`. T11 propagates that counter as an H2 source
gap and T13 can turn any affected path with no earlier decision into
`AMBIGUOUS / SOURCE_GAP_BEFORE_DECISION`.

That implication is not supported by the current public-data contract. Binance documents that
USD-M Futures Trades archives are sourced from `/fapi/v1/trades` and contain a `trade Id`, while
the official endpoint and public-data documentation do not promise that every integer ID must be
present. The accepted Trade Identity v2 contract already defines `venue_trade_id` as a preserved
exchange attribute, uses `(instrument, canonical_trade_id)` as historical fact identity and keeps
different official facts that share a venue ID.

The current official, checksum-bound archives show systematic numeric discontinuities without
missing days, file corruption, timestamp reversal or canonical identity loss. For
`2026-01-01` through `2026-07-03`:

| Instrument | Daily partitions | Public Trade rows | Skipped numeric IDs | Fraction of numeric ID span |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| BTCUSDT | 184 | 800,104,791 | 1,170,244 | 0.146048% |
| ETHUSDT | 184 | 1,255,571,071 | 1,811,220 | 0.144047% |

The small row-level discontinuity rate is amplified by the current any-discontinuity rule. In the
unpublished T15 P3/F3 primary T2 selection, all 705 BTC rows and all 1,151 ETH rows carry
`H2_VENUE_TRADE_ID_GAP`; 703 BTC and 1,148 ETH rows become source-gap AMBIGUOUS. This is diagnostic
evidence only, not a formal research result.

Primary source-contract references:

- [Binance public data README](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data#trades-1)
  identifies the USD-M Futures Trades source endpoint and exported fields;
- [Binance Futures Connector market endpoints](https://github.com/binance/binance-futures-connector-python/blob/main/binance/um_futures/market.py#L68-L125)
  documents recent, historical and aggregate trade lookup inputs without an integer-continuity
  guarantee.

## Proposed minimum correction

1. Preserve every existing Stage 1 row, archive Hash, Catalog, Manifest, quality report and
   published Stage 2 result unchanged.
2. Replace the single overloaded semantic with three explicit states in a new versioned derived
   contract:
   - `VENUE_ID_DISCONTINUITY_UNCORROBORATED`: numeric jump only; reportable anomaly, not by itself
     an H2 semantic gap;
   - `VERIFIED_PUBLIC_TRADE_GAP`: independent evidence proves a public Trade promised by the
     accepted source contract is absent;
   - `SOURCE_INTEGRITY_FAILURE`: missing/corrupt/truncated archive, checksum, Catalog, row-count or
     binding failure; fail closed.
3. Only `VERIFIED_PUBLIC_TRADE_GAP` that precedes the path decision may produce
   `AMBIGUOUS / SOURCE_GAP_BEFORE_DECISION`. An uncorroborated venue-ID discontinuity must remain
   visible in quality evidence and a conservative sensitivity projection but must not silently
   invalidate the main H2 path.
4. Require corroboration from at least one independent, immutable source-integrity fact before a
   numeric jump is promoted to `VERIFIED_PUBLIC_TRADE_GAP`. Acceptable evidence includes an
   official archive/checksum/manifest mismatch, a proven missing or truncated UTC partition, a
   Catalog-to-object row-count mismatch, or an approved official cross-source reconciliation that
   proves promised public Trade facts are absent. Absence of an integer ID alone is insufficient.
5. Preserve BTC and ETH separation and publish both the revised main result and the legacy
   any-discontinuity conservative bound. Never choose the interpretation from the observed event
   result.
6. After the audit and a separately approved affected-scope plan, create new append-only versions
   of the affected quality/path/label contracts and exactly scoped successor evidence. Do not
   rewrite or relabel existing Stage 1, T11, T12, T13, T14 or T15 artifacts in place.

## Required pre-implementation audit

Before changing code, publish a read-only audit that reports separately by instrument/year/month:

- official archive and checksum coverage;
- rows, canonical identities, duplicate/conflict counts and numeric discontinuities;
- discontinuity range sizes and adjacent event-time distances;
- missing/corrupt/truncated partitions and Catalog/object mismatches;
- T11 H2 windows intersecting uncorroborated discontinuities;
- T12 path-metric quality rows and summaries inherited from those T11 windows;
- T13 outcome changes under legacy versus proposed semantics;
- P1/P2/P3 and F0-F3 impact, with P3/F3 reported explicitly;
- whether an approved official cross-source check can corroborate any skipped numeric IDs.

The audit must not call a real account API, fabricate Trades, fill gaps or mutate accepted evidence.

## Invalidation and successor boundary

Approval of this CR would change an upstream H2 quality semantic. Therefore any new formal result
using the revised rule requires new contract versions and append-only successor evidence for every
affected downstream binding. At minimum, impact analysis must cover T11 path quality, T12
path-metric quality, T13 first-passage labels, T14 AMBIGUOUS bounds and T15 conditional-baseline
inputs/results. Existing
PASS records remain historically true under their declared contract but cannot be mixed with the
new semantic version.

This approval authorizes the required read-only audit only. It does not authorize implementation,
