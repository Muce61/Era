## Decision

For the bounded Stage 2 lifecycle only, Contract Price and canonical Trade paths are the approved
historical H3 price proxy. They must be labelled `CONTRACT_PRICE_H3_PROXY`; they are not historical
Mark Price and do not resolve the F1/live `workingType` decision.

T2 20bp remains an auxiliary First Passage observation and does not close the continuation policy.
The lifecycle target closes only when scenario terminal ticket equity reaches 20U. Under 800U
notional and the main 11bp round-trip cost this is approximately 136bp before funding. The exact
threshold is outcome-time dynamic because accumulated published funding is included.

## Unchanged gates

Historical funding evidence remains mandatory and cannot be replaced by zero. The approved
scenario liquidation boundary still requires a frozen bracket/scenario contract. OQ-S2-009/010/011,
the final-code seven-day rehearsal and the commit-bound human run receipt continue to block formal
Authority, bins, Run and publication.
