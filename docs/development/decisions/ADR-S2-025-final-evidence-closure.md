# ADR-S2-025 — Final evidence closure without research rescue

## Status

APPROVED — 2026-07-27

## Decision

`S2P17-T20` is an acceptance and packaging task, not a new research task. It consumes the formal
T19 decision and its transitive source bindings. Input, schema or Hash failure is
`FAILED_UNPUBLISHED`; otherwise the current research decision remains
`STAGE2_NO_GO_CURRENT_EVIDENCE`.

Real evidence cards are selected before their outcomes are read. The six strata are
BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT crossed with P1/P2/P3, and the lowest fixed-namespace SHA-256 identity wins each
stratum. SVG is the authoritative visual; PNG is a presentation preview. Neither can be
interpreted as a real return or execution claim.

T20 engineering PASS does not mean Stage 2 research PASS and cannot unlock Stage 3.
