## Delivered contract

- LONG signed move: `(path_price / fixed_reference_price - 1) * 10000`.
- MFE is non-negative and MAE is non-positive; persisted values use Decimal128 scale 18 with
  `ROUND_HALF_EVEN`, while extrema and threshold comparisons retain unquantized Decimal precision.
- H1 uses Contract-bar high/low and coarse-second timing. H2 uses Trade price in
  `(ts_event_ns, venue_trade_id, canonical_trade_id)` order while preserving historical fact
  identity `(instrument, canonical_trade_id)`.
- Equal extrema use the first stable occurrence. Time-since-MFE is last observation time minus the
  first final-MFE time.
- Pre-registered favorable-price activation thresholds are 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 50, 70 and
  100 bp. Time-to-Activation is measured from the left-closed window start. It is a historical
  price proxy, not a live-protection decision.
- No-observation paths retain null metrics and `NO_OBSERVATIONS`; gaps, truncation, ambiguity,
  quality and MarketEpisode lineage are propagated rather than repaired or hidden.
